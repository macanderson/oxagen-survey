Drawing rules

The agent survey drawing for a $100 OpenRouter API credit package. Effective 18 September 2026.

No purchase necessary. A purchase does not increase your chances of winning. Void where prohibited.

1. Sponsor

Oxagen Inc. ("Oxagen"), reachable at mac@oxagen.sh. OpenRouter is not a sponsor of, and is not affiliated with, this drawing.

2. Who may enter

Anyone 18 or older at the time of entry, except Oxagen employees and contractors and members of their households. Entry is void where the drawing is prohibited or restricted by law.

3. How to enter

  1. Complete the survey at the page that links here and submit it with a valid email address before the drawing closes.
  2. One entry per person and per email address. Submitting the survey again saves your new answers but does not add an entry.
  3. Free alternative entry: email mac@oxagen.sh with the subject "Survey drawing entry" and your name before the close date. That earns the same single entry.

4. Dates

Entries open on 18 September 2026 and close at 11:59 pm Eastern Time on 1 October 2026. The drawing takes place on 2 October 2026.

5. The prize

One prize: a $100.00 OpenRouter API credit package, delivered as credit applied to the winner's OpenRouter account or as an equivalent credit code, at Oxagen's choice, within 14 days of the winner's acceptance. Approximate retail value $100.00. The prize has no cash value, cannot be transferred, and cannot be substituted by the winner. OpenRouter's terms govern the use of the credit. Any taxes are the winner's responsibility.

6. How the winner is chosen

One entry is selected at random from all eligible entries by a computer program that uses a cryptographic random number generator, and the selection is recorded with its time, the total entry count and the index drawn. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries received.

7. Notification and acceptance

The winner is notified at the entered email address on the drawing date. The winner must reply within 7 days and may need to confirm eligibility. If the winner does not reply in time, is ineligible, or declines, another entry is drawn the same way.

8. Your data

Your email is used only to run the drawing and notify the winner, unless you ticked the box allowing one follow-up email. Survey answers are stored with your email, reported only in aggregate, and never sold. Write to mac@oxagen.sh to have your entry and answers deleted.

9. General

By entering you accept these rules and the sponsor's decisions, which are final. Oxagen may cancel or change the drawing if it cannot run as planned, and may disqualify entries that are automated, duplicated or fraudulent. Winner's first name and country may be announced with the winner's consent.
